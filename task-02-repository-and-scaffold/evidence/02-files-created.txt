## 2. Style Rules Audit & Enforcement

- **Zero Primitive Exposure:**
  - Raw color scales (e.g., `--palette-primary-40`, `--neutral-90`) are completely unreferenced in all styles.
  - Zero hardcoded color literals (`#...`, `rgb()`, `hsl()`) exist in any application styling or component markup.
- **Strict Semantic Token Application:**
  - Surfaces and Backgrounds: `var(--color-background)`, `var(--color-surface)`, `var(--color-surface-container)`, `var(--color-surface-container-low)`, `var(--color-surface-container-high)`, `var(--color-surface-container-lowest)`.
  - Typography and Text: `var(--color-on-background)`, `var(--color-on-surface)`, `var(--color-on-surface-variant)`, `var(--color-secondary)`.
  - Brand and Actions: `var(--color-primary)`, `var(--color-on-primary)`, `var(--color-primary-container)`, `var(--color-on-primary-container)`.
  - Tier Badges: `var(--color-secondary-container)` & `var(--color-on-secondary-container)` for free steps; `var(--color-primary-container)` & `var(--color-on-primary-container)` for PRO.
  - Borders: `var(--color-outline)`, `var(--color-outline-variant)`.
  - Elevation: `var(--shadow-soft)`, `var(--shadow-medium)`.
- **100vh Constraint:**
  - The viewport container is strictly clamped to `height: 100vh; max-height: 100vh; overflow: hidden;` via flex column distribution (header, main hero/catalog cards, footer status bar). No page scrollbars appear.
