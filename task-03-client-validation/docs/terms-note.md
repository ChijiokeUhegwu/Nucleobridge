# terms-note.md

I adapted the wording for the Terms and Conditions and Privacy Policy from an existing standard product legal template. 

I chose this approach because writing legal text from scratch without a legal background is not an honest or safe way to protect a business. It exposes the platform to massive liability and compliance failures. I also avoided asking an AI agent to generate the terms from scratch since it could hallucinate legal text without a verified structural baseline. 

By adapting a proven template, I ensured that standard baseline requirements—such as liability limitations, user data collection rules, and service usage policies—were properly structured before integrating them into the Nucleobridge application.
